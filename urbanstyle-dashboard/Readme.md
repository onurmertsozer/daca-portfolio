# Week 5: Visualisation Design — UrbanStyle Executive Dashboard 

## Business Task Created an interactive executive sales dashboard using Plotly and Streamlit for CEO Kristi Tamm and stakeholder alignment. 
## Deliverables &amp; Visuals 
- \*\*Peak Revenue Metric:\*\* December 2024 peak sales (€170,623). 
- \*\*Monthly Revenue Growth Trend:\*\* Line chart showing MoM growth trajectory. 
- \*\*Revenue by Product Category:\*\* Bar chart highlighting Footwear (€774.0k) and Men's Clothing (€749.8k) dominance. 
## Key Insights 
1\. \*\*Holiday Demand Spike:\*\* Revenue reached its annual peak in December (+54.3% MoM growth). 
2\. \*\*Core Revenue Drivers:\*\* Footwear and Men's Clothing generate the majority of total sales.
